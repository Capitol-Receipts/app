## Description
Capitol Receipts shows who funds every member of Congress, what stocks they trade, how they vote and who lobbies them, in plain English. Every number comes from public records, and every member is measured the same way.

LOOK UP ANYONE IN CONGRESS
Enter your ZIP code to find your two senators and your representative. Each member has one page with the money they raised for 2026, where it came from, their stock trades since January 2025, the votes they missed and how often they vote with their party.

EVERY STOCK TRADE
See each trade members and their families report, with the company, the date and the dollar range. Trades in industries a member's own committee oversees are flagged. Search any company to see who in Congress bought or sold it.

RANKINGS
Who trades the most, who raised the most, who relies most on small donors or PACs, who misses the most votes and who breaks with their party most often.

THE PRESIDENT AND CABINET
Stock trades reported by the President and Senate-confirmed officials on government ethics forms, plus the jobs, pay and ethics promises in their financial disclosures.

ALSO INSIDE
- Bills, votes and the groups that lobbied on them
- PACs, lobbying firms and companies
- 2026 House and Senate races and outside spending
- Past members of Congress back to 1789
- English and Spanish

CAPITOL RECEIPTS PRO
Pro sends an alert when someone you follow files a trade, watches the stocks you pick, flags every trade tied to a member's own committee, and adds CSV downloads. Pro is $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year, billed through your Apple ID.

Capitol Receipts is nonpartisan and independent. We take no money from parties, campaigns, PACs or lobbyists. Data comes from the Federal Election Commission, House and Senate disclosure reports, Voteview, the Senate lobbying database and the Office of Government Ethics.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
