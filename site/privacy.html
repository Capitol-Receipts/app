Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Who we are

Capitol Receipts is made by Squared Away Labs LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers www.capitolreceipts.com and app.capitolreceipts.com. Questions: admin@squaredawaylabs.com.

What we collect

If you just browse: standard usage data through Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, device type and rough location. No account needed.

If you create an account: your email address, the members and stocks you follow, your watchlist, your ZIP code if you enter one, and your alert settings.

If you subscribe to Pro: payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep your Stripe customer ID, your plan and its renewal date.

How we use it

To run your account, send the alerts and emails you ask for, process your subscription, keep the service secure and understand which parts of the app people use.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share who you follow, what you search or what's on your watchlist with advertisers, campaigns, parties or anyone else.

Who processes data for us

Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery) and Google Analytics (usage stats). Each handles data only to provide its service to us.

Your choices

You can change alert settings or cancel Pro any time in the app. To delete your account and its data, email admin@squaredawaylabs.com from the address on your account and we'll remove it within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax or legal reasons.

Children

Capitol Receipts isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and tell account holders by email.

iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. Until then, Capitol Receipts works in any browser on your phone. Open app.capitolreceipts.com and add it to your home screen.
