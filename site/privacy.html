Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

Who we are

Capitol Receipts is made by Squared Away Labs LLC ("we", "us"), Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania. This policy covers www.capitolreceipts.com, app.capitolreceipts.com and the Capitol Receipts iPhone and Android apps. Questions: admin@squaredawaylabs.com.

What we collect

If you just browse: usage data through Google Analytics, such as pages viewed, buttons tapped, device type, browser and approximate location from your IP address. It is not linked to an account. No account is needed to use the site.

If you create an account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google and they share it. We also keep what you save: your ZIP code or state if you enter one, the people, stocks and bills you follow, your alert settings, and when we last emailed you an alert.

If you sign in with Apple or Google: we receive your email address and an account identifier from them. For Sign in with Apple we also keep a token so we can tell Apple when you delete your account.

If you turn on alerts in the iPhone or Android app: a push notification token for your device, so we can send alerts to it. Signing out removes it.

If you subscribe to Pro: on the web, payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number; we keep your Stripe customer and subscription IDs, your plan and its renewal date. In the iPhone and Android apps, purchases go through Apple or Google Play, and RevenueCat confirms them to us; we keep your plan, its renewal date and the store’s transaction ID.

If you write to us or report a mistake: what you send, the email address you send it from, and, if you’re signed in, which account and plan it came from, so we can help without asking. We also keep a scrambled form of your network address for an hour to stop spam.

How we use it

To run your account, send the alerts and emails you ask for, process your subscription, keep the service secure, fix problems and understand which parts of the app people use.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell or rent your personal information. We don’t show ads. We don’t share who you follow, what you search or what’s on your watchlist with advertisers, campaigns, parties or anyone else.

Who processes data for us

Supabase (accounts and database), Vercel (hosting), Stripe (web payments), Apple (iPhone purchases, Sign in with Apple and iPhone push notifications), RevenueCat (confirming App Store and Google Play purchases), Google (Sign in with Google, Google Play purchases, Android push notifications through Firebase, and Google Analytics), Resend (email delivery) and GitHub (running our daily data updates, which don’t include your personal data). Each handles data only to provide its service to us.

Analytics

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. Visitors in Europe and the UK are asked first, and those cookies stay off unless they accept. Anywhere else you can block them with your browser’s settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site keeps working without them.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Alert history is overwritten as new alerts go out. Payment records that tax law requires us to keep, such as invoices in Stripe, are kept for as long as the law requires, even after you delete your account. Database backups are kept for a limited time and then expire.

Your choices

You can change your alert settings, turn off alerts or cancel Pro at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything saved with it, open Account and tap Delete my account; it happens right away. You can also ask us to delete it by email. To get a copy of your data or correct it, email admin@squaredawaylabs.com from the address on your account and we’ll reply within 30 days.

If you subscribe through Apple or Google Play, deleting your account doesn’t cancel the subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your account data is limited to you and to the few systems that need it to run the service, and our admin tools require two-step sign-in.

Children

Capitol Receipts isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the date above and tell account holders by email.
