About Capitol Receipts

Capitol Receipts shows who funds every member of Congress, what stocks they trade, how they vote and who lobbies them, in plain English. It’s free to use and built entirely from public records.

Who we are

Capitol Receipts is made by Squared Away Labs LLC, a small independent software studio based in Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania. We build and run the site, the app and the daily data updates ourselves.

Why we built it

The records that show how Congress is paid, how it trades and how it votes are public, but they are spread across a dozen government sites in formats few people have time to read. We put them in one place, match them to each member and write them up so anyone can check their own representatives in a minute.

Nonpartisan by design

Both parties appear on every list, and every member is measured the same way. We don’t endorse candidates or tell you what to think of a number. A high number doesn’t mean someone broke a rule: trading stock is legal, and missing a vote can have good reasons. We show the record so you can decide what to ask.

How we’re funded

By subscribers to Pro and by newsroom plans. We take no money from parties, campaigns, PACs or lobbyists, and we don’t sell your data. See our privacy policy.

Where the data comes from

The Federal Election Commission (campaign money), the House Clerk and the Secretary of the Senate (stock trades and financial disclosures), Voteview (every roll call vote since 1789), the Senate Lobbying Disclosure Act database (lobbying), the Office of Government Ethics (trades and finances of the President and Senate-confirmed officials), GovInfo and the Congressional Research Service (bills) and the Federal Judicial Center (judges). Here is how we count.

By the numbers

Today the site tracks 12,195 stock trades by members of Congress since January 2025, every current member’s campaign money and votes, and the trade reports of the President and cabinet. It updates every day.

Corrections

If something looks wrong, email corrections@capitolreceipts.com with a link to the page. We check it against the original filing, fix confirmed errors quickly and note the correction.

Contact

General questions: admin@squaredawaylabs.com. Reporters and researchers: see Newsrooms.
