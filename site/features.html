★ The tour

Everything they file. Nothing you have to decode.

Every way Capitol Receipts turns government paperwork into something you can read on the bus. Every screen below uses real data.

History

Every member since 1789.

Over 12,000 past members of Congress, past appointees, every president and vice president, and stories from the archives. Rep. John Dingell served 59 years, the longest ever.

Biggest traders, party rebels, ethics actions and close votes

Profiles

Three numbers. Then what stands out.

Every member gets an official portrait, votes missed, money raised and stock trades up top. Rep. Gil Cisneros's profile leads with 1,646 trades since January 2025, more than anyone in Congress.

Money

Who's paying for the campaign.

Small donors versus big donors versus PACs, the industries behind the money, the companies donors work for, and outside spending. Sen. Bernie Sanders raised $24.9M, 69% of it from people giving under $200.

Top PAC donors and money by industry
Every cycle since 2020, linked to FEC filings
Stock trades

Buys, sells, and the ones that look too close.

Each trade with the company, the amount and the date. We match every company to its industry using SEC filings and flag trades that fall under the member's own committees. Rep. Lisa McClain has 253 of them.

House and Senate, 12,917 trades and counting
Links to every original report
Votes

Who shows up. Who goes rogue.

Attendance month by month, party loyalty on votes where the parties split, recent votes, and how they voted on the bills lobbyists fought over most. Sort all 539 members by any of it.

Rankings for 13 different measures
Filter by party, chamber or state
Lobbying & ethics

The revolving door, named.

Lobbyists have to list the government jobs they held. We match them to the members they worked for. 943 former staffers are now registered lobbyists, 18 of them from Sen. John Cornyn's office.

Who they lobby for now
House Ethics Committee statements, in their own words
Appointees

The people Congress confirms.

582 Senate-confirmed appointees with the vote count, who crossed party lines, and their ethics filings. Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth was confirmed 50–50 and reported $4.6M in salary from Fox News the year before.

Jobs and pay before government
What they promised to sell, and any conflict waivers
New

More receipts, everywhere you look.

The 2026 elections, every company and PAC, a radar for suspicious trades and alerts that land the morning a report is filed.

2026 elections

Every race on the ballot.

All 435 House races and 35 Senate races: the candidates, what each has raised and spent, and how much outside groups are pouring in. Results show here after the polls close.

Conflict radar

Trades worth a second look.

A running list of new trades in industries a member’s own committees oversee, plus every trade over $250K. Filter it, or download it as a spreadsheet.

Pro

Know the morning it’s filed.

An email the morning a trade is reported, a watchlist for any stock, and downloads. Free for 7 days, then $4.99 a month or $39 a year. Every record stays free for everyone.

Companies

Look up any company.

Its lobbying, what its PAC gives to Congress, its federal contracts, and which members trade its stock. Lockheed Martin received $88.3B in contracts in two years.

PACs and lobbying

Follow the PAC money.

Every PAC ranked by what it gives, filtered by type: company PACs, unions, ideological groups and more. Plus every lobbying group, every lobbying firm and the bills they lobby on.

Judges

The courts, too.

Every federal judge since 1789, who appointed them, how the Senate voted and their careers. Also: committees, caucuses, ethics reviews and outside spending groups.

On a computer

Big lists, on a big screen.

On a laptop or desktop, the longest lists (PACs, companies, the most-lobbied bills) become ranked tables you can scan top to bottom, with the menu always on the side.
