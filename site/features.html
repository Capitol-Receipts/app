What the app shows you.

Capitol Receipts puts campaign filings, stock trade reports, votes and lobbying records into short pages you can read on your phone. Every screen below shows real data.

History

Every member since 1789.

Over 12,000 past members of Congress, past appointees, every president and vice president, and stories from the archives. Rep. John Dingell served 59 years, the longest ever.

Biggest traders, party rebels, ethics actions and close votes
Your own senators and representative, one tap away
Profiles

Votes missed, money and trades up top.

Every member gets an official portrait, votes missed, money raised and stock trades up top. Rep. Gil Cisneros's profile leads with 1,646 trades since January 2025, more than anyone in Congress.

Money

Who's paying for the campaign.

Small donors versus big donors versus PACs, the industries behind the money, the companies donors work for, and outside spending. Sen. Bernie Sanders raised $24.9M, 69% of it from people giving under $200.

Stock trades

Every trade, with the ones tied to their committees flagged.

Each trade with the company, the amount and the date. We match every company to its industry using SEC filings and flag trades that fall under the member's own committees. Rep. Lisa McClain has 253 of them.

Pro

Get an email when a trade is filed.

Same-day trade emails, watchlists for stocks and bills, each member’s stock picks vs the S&P 500, a Friday briefing and downloads. Free for 14 days, then $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Every record stays free for everyone.

Companies

Its lobbying, what its PAC gives to Congress, its federal contracts, and which members trade its stock. Lockheed Martin received $88.3B in contracts in two years.

PACs and lobbying

Every PAC, ranked.

Every PAC ranked by what it gives, filtered by type: company PACs, unions, ideological groups and more. Plus every lobbying group, every lobbying firm and the bills they lobby on.

Judges

Every federal judge.

Every federal judge since 1789, who appointed them, how the Senate voted and their careers. Also: committees, caucuses, ethics reviews and outside spending groups.

Common questions

Can I get an alert when a member trades stock?

Yes. With Pro, you get an email when a member you follow files a new trade report.

Does it cover both the House and the Senate?

Yes. Every current senator and representative, plus every past member since 1789.

Can I see stock trades by the President and cabinet?

Yes. Officials’ stock trades lists every OGE Form 278-T report posted since January 2025.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in any phone browser, and you can add it to your home screen. iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

Can I download the data?

Pro includes CSV downloads of trades, money and votes. Newsroom plans add bulk access for teams.

Is it available in Spanish?

Member, state, district, election and stock pages are available in Spanish. Ver en español.
