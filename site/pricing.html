Questions

Is the free version actually useful?

Yes. Every profile, every record, every party page and the full compare tool are free. Pro adds same-day alerts, stock and bill watchlists, stock picks vs the S&P 500, the conflict radar, a weekly briefing and downloads.

Do I need a card for the free trial?

Yes. Add a card at checkout to start your 14 free days. You won’t be charged until the trial ends, and if you cancel before then you pay nothing.

Is there an iPhone or Android app?

Not yet. iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. Capitol Receipts already works in any phone browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. Your account and Pro plan will carry over to the apps.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in two taps. If you cancel during the free trial, you pay nothing.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell or share who you follow or what you search.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Capitol Receipts runs in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen like any app.
