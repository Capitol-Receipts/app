Questions

Is the free version actually useful?

Yes. Every profile, every record, every party page and the full compare tool are free. Pro adds daily alerts, a stock watchlist, the conflict radar and downloads.

Do I need a card for the free week?

Yes. Add a card at checkout to start your 7 free days. You won’t be charged until the week ends, and if you cancel before then you pay nothing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in two taps. If you cancel during the free trial, you pay nothing.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell or share who you follow or what you search.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Capitol Receipts runs in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen like any app.
