Common questions

How do I cancel Pro?

If you subscribed on the website, open the Pro screen and tap Manage. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. If you subscribed in the iPhone app, cancel in your iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions. If you subscribed in the Android app, open Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

I paid but don’t see Pro.

Make sure you’re signed in with the same email you used to pay. In the iPhone or Android app, open Account and tap Restore purchases. Still missing? Send us a message below and include the email on the receipt.

Can I get a refund?

For web subscriptions, send us a message below and we’ll sort it out. Purchases made in the iPhone app are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Android purchases by Google in Google Play.

I’m not getting alert emails.

Check Account › Alert settings in the app and make sure email alerts are on. Look in your spam or promotions folder, and add alerts@capitolreceipts.com to your contacts. Free accounts get a weekly update for up to 5 people they follow. Pro sends alerts within hours of new filings.

I can’t sign in.

On the sign-in screen at app.capitolreceipts.com, tap Forgot password to get a reset link. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use the same button again; it signs you in to the same account.

A number looks wrong.

Stock trades are reported in ranges, and members can file up to 45 days after a trade, so recent trades can appear late. If something still looks wrong, send it below with a link to the page, or tap Report a mistake on that page in the app. We check it against the original filing and list confirmed fixes on the corrections page.

How often is the data updated?

Several times every weekday, from the House, the Senate, the Federal Election Commission and the other public sources listed on How it works.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account in the app and tap Delete my account. It happens right away. More about deleting your account.

Send us a message

A person reads every message. We usually reply within one business day.

Other ways to reach us

Corrections to the data: corrections@capitolreceipts.com. Reporters and researchers: see Newsrooms. Capitol Receipts is made by Squared Away Labs LLC, Wilkes-Barre, Pennsylvania.
