# Capitol Receipts: performance, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and responsive audit

Date: 2026-10-26. Read-only. Live sites tested: app.capitolreceipts.com (build `APP_VER=202610261459`), www.capitolreceipts.com. About 40 normal page loads in total, run one at a time. No load testing.
Scripts and raw output are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude/5941ef03-c418-5546-a338-6ac77d8d5bda/scratchpad/audit/perf/`: `lh.sh` and `lh/*.json` (Lighthouse), `pw.js` and `out_*.json` (Playwright), `obs.js` (throttled PerformanceObserver), `tb.js` (tab bar).

## Test conditions

| Run | Tool | Device | CPU | Network | Cache |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lighthouse mobile | Lighthouse 12 (npm), headless Chromium 141 | Moto G Power emulation, 412x823 | 4x slowdown (simulated) | Simulated slow 4G: 150 ms RTT, 1.6 Mbps down | Cold. New profile each run, service worker never installed |
| Lighthouse desktop | `--preset=desktop` | 1350x940 | 1x | 40 ms RTT, 10 Mbps | Cold |
| PerformanceObserver ("obs") | Playwright plus CDP | 412x823, mobile, DPR 1.75 | 4x (`Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate`) | Applied with devtools: 150 ms latency, 1.6 Mbps down, 750 kbps up | Cold, service worker blocked |
| Payload, navigation, memory, axe, keyboard, responsive | Playwright 1.5x, Chromium 141 | 390x844 mobile, plus the widths listed | 1x (typing test also at 4x) | Unthrottled | Cold, service worker blocked |

All traffic went through the sandbox egress proxy, which adds about 100–250 ms of round-trip time. Lighthouse's simulated throttling partly corrects for this. Numbers are lab estimates, not field data.

**Navigation and memory** (app, 390 px): member to stock made 0 duplicate requests. Opening a stock page pulls `companies` (36 KiB) plus about 20 small portraits (1–2 KiB each). No polling was seen in a 15 s idle window after load. Over 20 in-app navigations, measured after forced GC: JS heap 52 MB at start, then **116 MB** once the history data loaded, then flat (116.0 → 116.8 MB). DOM nodes 850–1,300, listeners 118–127. **No leak**, but the steady-state heap is large. Typing in search re-renders all of `#app` on each keystroke: 17–55 ms per keystroke at 1x, 58–79 ms at 4x CPU. That is acceptable.

## Not verified
- Real Safari/WebKit and the iOS Capacitor WebView: only Chromium was tested. iOS memory limits for the WebView are not verified (see PERF-01).
- Screen-reader output with VoiceOver or NVDA. Only the DOM and ARIA were inspected.
- Lighthouse INP: lab runs measure TBT only. INP was estimated from long tasks (2.0 s worst).
- Repeat-visit performance with the service worker active (`serviceWorkers: 'block'` was used everywhere).
- Spanish (`es`) bundle sizes: `es_dict` and `glossary_es` were excluded because the English default was tested.
