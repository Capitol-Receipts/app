Capitol Receipts pulls together public records on every member of Congress so you can see, in plain English:

  • who gives money to each member of Congress, down to the PACs and industries
  • the stocks they buy and sell, including trades in industries their own committees oversee
  • how they vote, how many votes they miss, and how often they break with their party
  • who lobbies them and on which bills

It covers all 539 current members, and every past member back to 1789. The data comes from the FEC, House and Senate disclosures, roll-call records and lobbying filings, and it updates every morning. Everyone is measured the same way, regardless of party.

A good place to start: put in your ZIP code to find your two senators and your House member, then follow them. Free accounts can follow up to 5 people and get a weekly email about what they did. If you want those updates daily, plus alerts on specific stocks, you can try Pro free for a week from the app.
